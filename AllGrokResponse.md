### Letters to Federal Agencies and Divisions Regarding Violations by Illinois HB 3637

Below are formal complaint letters to key federal agencies and divisions, focusing solely on factual violations of federal laws, regulations, the U.S. Constitution, and President Trump's 2025 Executive Orders incurred by Illinois House Bill 3637 (signed August 22, 2025, effective January 1, 2026). The letters are generic, with the sender as "Concerned Citizen of Illinois" to maintain anonymity. Each letter lists specific violations with citations, requests investigation and enforcement, and is dated September 26, 2025. Send via certified mail or official online forms for tracking.

#### 1. Letter to U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

Concerned Citizen of Illinois   [Your Address, if desired; otherwise omit]   September 26, 2025    Office of Compliance   Center for Drug Evaluation and Research   Food and Drug Administration   10903 New Hampshire Ave   Silver Spring, MD 20993    Re: Complaint Regarding Illinois HB 3637 – Violations of FD&C Act Preemption and Related Federal Laws    Dear Office of Compliance:    Illinois House Bill 3637 (HB 3637), signed August 22, 2025 and effective January 1, 2026, amends the Illinois Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to exempt drugs whose FDA approval was revoked (for drugs approved before January 1, 2025) from being deemed "adulterated" if recommended by the World Health Organization (WHO). This provision remains in effect until 2035. HB 3637 incurs direct violations of federal law and policy, as detailed below. I request an immediate investigation, guidance to preempt enforcement, and referral to the Department of Justice if necessary.    **Violations of Federal Laws and Regulations:**   - **Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FD&C Act, 21 U.S.C. § 301 et seq.)**:     - § 331(d): Prohibits introducing unapproved new drugs into interstate commerce. HB 3637 violates this by permitting revoked FDA-approved drugs to be used based on WHO recommendation, allowing what federal law forbids.     - § 355: Requires FDA approval for new drugs based on safety and efficacy data. HB 3637 bypasses this for WHO-recommended drugs, creating a conflicting state standard preempted under 21 CFR Part 808 (no exemptions apply).   - **Public Health Service Act (PHSA, 42 U.S.C. § 201 et seq.)**:     - § 262: Regulates biologics and requires FDA approval. HB 3637 violates this by allowing biologics without FDA approval if WHO-recommended, interfering with federal public health protections.   - **Administrative Procedure Act (APA, 5 U.S.C. § 551 et seq.)**: HB 3637 undermines FDA's APA-compliant rulemaking for drug approvals by creating state rules that conflict with federal processes.    **Violations of U.S. Constitution:**   - **Supremacy Clause (U.S. Const. art. VI, cl. 2)**: Federal law preempts conflicting state laws. HB 3637 conflicts with the FD&C Act and PHSA, triggering conflict, field, and obstacle preemption (*Buckman Co. v. Plaintiffs’ Legal Committee*, 531 U.S. 341 (2001); *Geier v. American Honda Motor Co.*, 529 U.S. 861 (2000)).   - **Commerce Clause (U.S. Const. art. I, § 8, cl. 3)**: HB 3637 burdens interstate commerce by creating inconsistent drug standards (*Healy v. Beer Institute*, 491 U.S. 324 (1989)).    **Violations of President Trump's 2025 Executive Orders:**   - **EO 14155: Withdrawing the United States from the World Health Organization (Jan. 20, 2025)**: Directs withdrawal from the WHO and cessation of negotiations on WHO agreements. HB 3637 violates this by deferring to WHO recommendations over FDA standards.   - **Regulatory Freeze Pending Review (Jan. 21, 2025)**: Halts new regulations across agencies, including FDA; HB 3637's state override conflicts with this freeze on disruptive rules.   - **Lowering Drug Prices by Once Again Putting Americans First (EO 14273, April 15, 2025)**: Reinforces FDA oversight for drug access and safety. HB 3637 bypasses FDA for WHO-recommended drugs, undermining this EO.   - **EO on Domestic Pharmaceutical Manufacturing (May 5, 2025)**: Directs FDA to strengthen oversight of foreign-influenced drugs. HB 3637 elevates WHO (foreign entity) over FDA, violating this policy.    These violations warrant FDA action to prevent HB 3637's enforcement, including issuing a preemptive advisory or coordinating with DOJ.    Sincerely,   Concerned Citizen of Illinois
